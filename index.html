My Unofficial Guide to Navigating a Fantasy World

The Creatures

Dragons

  • Very large creatures
  • Stay clear of its path if it flys overhead
  • Do not attack the creatures even if they provoke you

Elves

  • Are in close relations to humans
  • Remain very secluded
  • Will not provoke unless threatened

Mermaids

  • Will live in water for the most part
  • Are very solitary and peaceful creatures
  • Do not confuse them with sierns

The Very-Great Kingdom

People

  • They are of your blood and relation. Treat them with respect as if they are your family
  • They are mostly merchants, craftsman, and blacksmiths
  • Will be willing to help during your quest

Spell Casters

  • They will not be seen during the day and keep themselves secret in covens
  • To let them help you on your journey, you must befriend one of them or become thier Apprentice
  • Uses anicent magic from the Old Kingdom

The King and Queen

  • Mysterious figures that rule over the kingdom you reside in. What secrets do they hide?
  • Only at the end of your quest will you be able to attend an audience with them
  • As a means of support, they will provide you with a horse, sword, and money to continue your journey

Your Quest

The Reason

  • Your village has been pillaged by a mysterious force
  • Food is running out and there is no one that can help
  • You must travel to the source of the mysterious force to free the village from its suffering

The Obstacles

  • You will face forces from the mysterious darkness that has taken your village
  • You will perhaps make new allies and enemies that will either help or hinder your journey
  • Bonus: might be able to become a spellcaster

You never know where your journey might lead you
